What is SearchBook and How does it work?

Every time you open a website, your request travels to a real machine somewhere in the world and back, usually in a fraction of a second. We rarely think about where that machine is or how far the signal goes. SearchBook makes that journey visible.

Enter any URL, and SearchBook traces the site back to its server. It shows you the IP address, the city and country where the server sits, and an estimate of how far your ping traveled based on how long the round trip took. A site that feels local might be hosted across the country, and one that feels global might be just down the road.

The more you search, the bigger your map of the internet becomes. Your profile keeps a running total of the distance your pings have covered, the number of pings you've sent, and how long you've been exploring.
